# Required Materials (not final)
**Date:** 9/17/2026

- Forskolin (10 mg)
- IBMX (50 mg)
- DMNB-caged cAMP **OR** DEACM-caged cAMP
  **Note:** I could not find a vendor that sells DMNB-caged cAMP; DEACM-cAMP is an alternative, but it has a much higher quantum yield than DMNB-cAMP and requires different loading and caging parameters:
  - For DMNB-caged cAMP: 340–365 nm light
  - For DEACM-caged cAMP: 400–405 nm light
- [cAMP-dependent protein kinase A catalytic subunit](https://www.promega.com/products/cell-signaling/kinase-assays-and-kinase-biology/camp_dependent-protein-kinase_-catalytic-subunit/?tabset0=0) (Promega, 1 × 2,500 u)
- [Kemptide PKA peptide substrate](https://www.promega.com/products/cell-signaling/kinase-assays-and-kinase-biology/kemptide-_pka_-peptide-substrate/?tabset0=0) (Promega, 10 mg/mL)
- [ATP](https://biofargo.com/products/adenosine-5-triphosphate-atp-100mm-solution?variant=45362517737653) 100 mM stock solution
- Synthetic [Phospho-Ser5]kemptide for the standard curve (50 mg)
  - For low input phosphoproteomics during phases 3–5
- Western blot antibodies: anti-CREB, anti-pCREB (Ser133), anti-PKA pan-substrate, anti-VASP (Ser157)
